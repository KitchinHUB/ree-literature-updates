## 3. The four raw URLs

### `https://www.osti.gov/etdeweb/servlets/purl/20176396` (line ~574, "TOPO Systems")

**Identified, but it does not support the claim it was attached to — and it explains a
separate misattribution.** The PDF is Charbonnel, Flandin, Presson & Morel (2000),
*Thermodynamics properties of complexation and extraction of Ln(III) by diamides*,
Atalante 2000, Avignon, paper P3.29, INIS-FR-300, OSTI ID 20176396. It is about the
DIAMEX process and malonamide **diamides**. I read all four pages:

- It contains **nothing about TOPO** and nothing about Eu(NO₃)₃/Am(NO₃)₃/Cm(NO₃)₃.
  The "TOPO Systems" table (ΔH ≈ 29 kJ/mol constant La–Er, slight decrease Tm–Lu,
  complete ΔG/ΔH/ΔS sets for Eu, Am and Cm nitrates) was unsourced. I searched
  CrossRef for a real source and found none. **The TOPO subsection is deleted**, and
  so is the `TOPO/Ln³⁺` row of the "Summary of Thermodynamic Trends" table.
- It *does* contain, verbatim, the four numbers the chapter was printing in its
  **"HDEHP/D2EHPA Systems"** table: "ΔextH equal to −35 kJ/mol for La(III) and to
  −27 kJ/mol for Yb(III), from HNO₃ 3 mol/L medium … In the case of the actinide ion
  Am(III), the ΔextH value was found equal to −36 kJ/mol", together with the
  "increase along the Ln(III) series" trend. Those are **DMDBTDMA (a malonamide
  diamide) van't Hoff values, not HDEHP values.** The table has been moved into a new
  `#### Malonamide Diamide (DMDBTDMA) Systems` subsection, given its real conditions
  (0.5 M DMDBTDMA in TPH, La/Nd/Eu/Er/Yb/Am nitrates from 3 M HNO₃) and cited as
  `[@charbonnel2000thermodynamics]`. The `DMDBTDMA/Ln³⁺` row replaces the TOPO row in
  the summary table, and the `HDEHP/Ln³⁺` row lost the borrowed numbers.
- The unsourced "entropy-enthalpy compensation … less compact hydration zones of
  light lanthanides are disrupted more readily" sentence was replaced with the
  entropy explanation the INL report actually gives (complex ordering plus
  rehydration of the three exchanged protons).

New key: **`charbonnel2000thermodynamics`** (`@inproceedings`).

### `https://inis.iaea.org/records/5cbkn-fw635` (line ~588, DGA systems)

**Resolved cleanly.** The INIS record is Ansari, Pathak, Husain, Prasad, Parmar &
Manchanda (2006), *Extraction of actinides using N,N,N',N'-tetraoctyl diglycolamide
(TODGA): a thermodynamic study*, Radiochimica Acta **94**(6-7), 307–312. Verified
against CrossRef (`10.1524/ract.2006.94.6.307`) with the required User-Agent header:
DOI, author list, journal, volume, issue, pages and year all confirmed. The abstract
states in terms that "the extraction processes of Am(III) and U(VI) are enthalpy driven
whereas entropy factor counteracts the extraction. However, in the case of Pu(IV), the
extraction process is enthalpy as well as entropy favoured" — i.e. it supports exactly
the two bullets it was standing behind, which now carry `[@ansari2006extraction]`
individually rather than a URL in a reference list.

New key: **`ansari2006extraction`** (`@article`). Distinct from the existing
`ansari2011chemistry` (Chem. Rev. diglycolamide review) and `ansari2016thermodynamics`
(Dalton Trans. tripodal DGA calorimetry), both already in `references.bib`.

### `https://nash.chem.wsu.edu/publications/` (line ~652, "Nash Group Publications")

**Deleted.** A pointer to a research group's publication list is not a reference for
any claim. The `### Key Research Groups & Resources` section (a "here are some groups"

No new key needed.

### `https://inldigitallibrary.inl.gov/sites/sti/sti/4781579.pdf` (line ~660, INL)

**Identified; two of its three bullets were wrong and were removed.** The PDF is
Martin, L. R. & Zalupski, P. R. (2010), *Thermodynamics and Kinetics of Advanced
Separations Systems — FY 2010 Summary Report*, INL/EXT-10-19986,
FCR&D-SEPA-2010-000174, Idaho National Laboratory, September 2010. I read it:

- "Thermodynamics and kinetics of advanced separations" — supported (it is the title).
- "**HDEHP, TOPO, and DGA systems**" — **not supported**. The report is HDEHP/TALSPEAK
  throughout; `TOPO`, `diglycolamide` and `DGA` do not appear. Bullet removed.
- "First transplutonium calorimetric measurements" — supported, in the report's own
  words: "the first account of a direct calorimetric measurement of the heat of
  liquid-liquid partitioning of any transuranic element", for Am(III) from pH 3.2,
  1.0 M NaNO₃ into 0.2 M HDEHP in *n*-dodecane. That claim was previously sitting in
  the "Two-Phase Calorimetry" bullet list attributed to `@zalupski2008two`, which is
  the 2008 **lanthanide** paper and does not contain it; it now cites
  `@martin2010thermodynamics` and carries the actual conditions.

The report also supplied the replacement text for the HDEHP subsection: exothermic
heat of transfer opposed by a negative entropy term (complex ordering + rehydration of
three exchanged protons), and Am(III) falling on the lanthanide ΔG/ΔH/ΔS trend lines
at the position expected for Pm(III), consistent with electrostatic binding to a hard
donor.

New key: **`martin2010thermodynamics`** (`@techreport`).

## Number conflicts found and how they were resolved

- **63 % vs 74 % vs 339/1,296 (old `14:67-69`).** This was the review's flagged
  contradiction. I pulled the full text (EuropePMC, PMC11558677) and it is not a
  contradiction — the book had merged two different results. The 74 % is the
  live Th campaign: 339 measurements (113 unique conditions × triplicate) against
  an estimated 1,296 (432 conditions × triplicate) for a full grid. The 63 % is a
  *retrospective, virtual* campaign on a literature UO₂²⁺/DEHiBA dataset used to
  develop the method, where the optimizer found the maximum D(U) in at most 90
  experiments out of a 244-point space. The old phrasing, "identified optimal
  conditions using only 63 % of experiments", inverted the sense of that figure
  (it is a 63 % *saving*, i.e. ≤37 % of the experiments). Both numbers are now in
  the text, each attached to the campaign it belongs to.
- **SAFE dataset composition.** Pass 1 said "11 actinide/lanthanide isotopes",
  pass 2 said "11 different actinides and lanthanides". The paper says "2132
  distribution ratios ... involving 35 unique monoamides and 11 different
  actinides". I used the paper's wording and added its caveat that the 2,132 are
  unique reported conditions, not replicate-corrected.
- **Liu 2022 training-set size.** Pass 1 "1,202 log D values"; pass 2 "Thousands
  of experimental D values". 1,202 is confirmed by the bib abstract; the vague
  restatement is gone. Same for "R² = 0.85 on validation" vs "R² > 0.85 for
  held-out test" — kept 0.85.
- **200 vs 100-200+ measurements/day** is not a conflict (platform spec vs field
  target); both are kept, now adjacent and labelled as such.

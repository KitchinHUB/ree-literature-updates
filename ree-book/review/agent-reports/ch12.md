The scCO₂ material is untouched. The membrane stub (old lines 27–54) was replaced
by a section with this structure:

- `## Membrane Separation Technologies` — opening that separates liquid membranes
  (geometry is the hardware, the carrier does the chemistry) from pressure-driven
  filtration (size plus charge). `[@kaczorowska2023latest]`, `[@bashiri2022rare]`,
  `[@kujawa2023membrane]`, `[@chen2018overview]`.
- `### Liquid Membranes: The Cascade Folded into One Barrier` — carrier chemistry
  is the same acidic organophosphorus extractant chemistry as ch. 3 (D2EHPA,
  PC88A/EHEHPA), cross-referenced to `[](#solvent-extraction-fundamentals)`;
  extraction, diffusion and stripping happening simultaneously across one
  barrier; HFSLM lumen/shell geometry; and the pH gradient as the driving force,
  which is what lets the transport run *against* the REE concentration gradient
  `[@middleton2023separation]`. Dilute-feed relevance cross-referenced to
  `[](#ion-adsorption-clays)`.
- `#### What Has Actually Been Demonstrated` — the two results relocated from
  ch. 9 (see below), plus the Nd > Pr > Dy transport ordering
  `[@kaczorowska2023latest]` and the Nd case where extraction reached 99.80% but
  stripping only 78.58% `[@mohdee2023applicability]`, which is the honest picture
  of where these systems lose material.
- `### Nanofiltration and Ultrafiltration` — Donnan, dielectric and size
  exclusion, and why complexation is what actually buys rejection
  `[@feng2026selective; @bashiri2022rare]`; then a six-row table of real numbers:
  NF-300 Nd(III) rejection 86.7% → 99.5% with SDS / 99.4% with EDTA
  `[@murthy2011application]`; Desal G10 Gd(III) <10% without DTPA, 5–95% with it
  `[@sorin2005rejection]`; NF270 on acid mine drainage `[@lopez2018application]`;
  micellar-enhanced UF at 97% on synthetic leachate but 71% on the real one
  `[@kosemutlu2020separation]`; polymer-enhanced UF >90% `[@duan2015removal]`;
  ceramic MEUF ~99% for Y *and* for Zn `[@innocenzi2018treatment]`. Then flux and
  pressure (12–24 bar, economic optimum at 12 bar / pH 3.5
  `[@kosemutlu2018application]`), the NF270 acid-ageing limit (1 M H₂SO₄, four
  weeks `[@lopez2018application]`), and the plain statement that no NF or UF
  membrane has separated adjacent lanthanides `[@feng2026selective]`.
- `### Electrodialysis` — kept verbatim from the original.
- `### Why Membranes Have Not Displaced the Cascade` — the section the chapter was
  missing, in four parts, not softened:
  - **Carrier loss.** D2EHPA/PC88A aqueous solubility of 5–10 ppm (ch. 3's own
    number) against a pore inventory measured in millilitres; the leak that is
    invisible in a mixer-settler is the whole inventory here
    `[@kemperman1996stability]`.
  - **Lifetime.** The 8 m² pilot's HFSLM decayed rapidly; gel/third-phase
    thresholds of 46% Nd, 38% Y, 46% Dy, 65% Er loading; hours and days against
    the years a mixer-settler runs `[@alemrajabi2022separation]`.
  - **Real leachates.** Fe(III) tolerance is >100× lower than Al or Fe(II)
    `[@middleton2023separation]`, and the real-vs-synthetic MEUF gap
    `[@kosemutlu2020separation]`. Stated explicitly as carrier competition, *not*
    fouling — in that study excess Fe(III) produced no observable fouling layer.
    I did not write "fouling" where the evidence says competition.
  - **And the selectivity was never the point.** β ≈ 2–4 for adjacent light pairs,
    same as SX. The membrane collapses the contactor, not the stage count. Closes
    with the genuine niche: one group separation out of a dilute, dirty feed,
    run against the concentration gradient.

## B8 — lanmodulin

The ch. 12 bullet claimed a single lanmodulin selectivity figure that silently
merged two different comparisons. Rewritten to say: ~10⁸-fold is Ln **over
calcium**, a group separation; the monomer binds La through Lu at nearly the same
picomolar affinity, so intra-series discrimination is weak; engineered dimers do
fractionate, but the ratios usually quoted for them are **La against Dy**, the two
ends of the series, not an adjacent pair. Cross-referenced to
`[](#biological-and-biomimetic-separations)`.

I deliberately quoted **no number** for the intra-series case here. My brief said
the monomer Kd varies ~25-fold across La→Lu; ch. 11, already fixed by another
agent, says the monomer shows "about fivefold" light-over-heavy preference,
cited to `@cotruvo2023enhanced`. Rather than plant a third number and create a
new cross-chapter contradiction, ch. 12 states the qualitative fact and points at
ch. 11. **Someone should reconcile 25-fold vs fivefold** — they may be measuring
different things (full-series Kd spread vs a light/heavy group ratio), but the
book should not contain both unexplained.

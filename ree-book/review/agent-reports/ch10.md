## Claims changed because I could not source them

- **LnBO₃ / CaCO₃ isomorphism bullets (old 367–370).** "LaBO₃, PrBO₃, NdBO₃ →
  aragonite; SmBO₃ → H-form; TbBO₃, EuBO₃, GdBO₃, DyBO₃, YBO₃ → vaterite" is
  *not* in the borate paper (Yin 2017 is about hydrated polyborate phases, not
  simple orthoborates) and I could not find a source for the per-element
  assignment. Replaced with one softened sentence citing Bradley, Graf & Roth
  (1966), whose title verifies only that vaterite-type LnBO₃ exist. The
  per-element list is gone.
- **"Strong preference for incorporating smaller lanthanide ions" (M₄L₄).** The
  Li 2018 abstract does not state a direction of size preference. Replaced with
  what the abstract does support: cages form with Ca²⁺, Cd²⁺ and the full Ln³⁺
  series, and self-sort with high precision (which the M₂L₃ analogues do not).
- **"Introduction of hydrophobic alkyl groups enables liquid-liquid extraction
  with water" (M₄L₄).** Not supported by anything I could verify. Deleted;
  replaced with the abstract-supported statement that the authors offer this as
  a design principle for next-generation extractants.
- **"Four structurally distinct lanthanide compounds … from the same
  DMF/H₂O/TFA solvent system."** The count "four" and the TFA component are not
  in the Yin 2025 abstract. Softened to "structurally distinct lanthanide
  compounds … from the same mixed-organic solvent system", cited.
- **"as seen in Nd/Dy systems"** (reaction-time bullet) corrected to the Nd/Sm
  borate system, which is the pair the abstract attributes to kinetic control;
  Nd/Dy is the flotation case.

Numbers I *did* keep are exact matches to the source abstracts: six borate
phases and the one-step Nd/Dy density flotation (`yin2017rare`); La/Ce 2.0 ± 0.1,
La/Sm 8.9 ± 0.1, La/Lu 26.9 ± 3.1 (`yin2025selective`); >5 orders of magnitude
La³⁺ vs Gd³⁺ and SF up to 45 for Ce/La with HDEHP (`thiele2020tuning`); 99.7 %
Nd from an automotive magnet (`jones2025macrocyclic`, newly added, verified).

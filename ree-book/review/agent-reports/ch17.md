### 1. CAPEX/OPEX share tables (was ~67-88) — DELETED, replaced with prose

Two tables gone. Numbers no longer claimed:

- CAPEX: process equipment 30-40%, installation 15-20%, engineering 10-15%,

- OPEX: reagents 40-60%, energy 15-25%, labor 10-20%, maintenance 5-10%,

### 2. Mountain Pass cost table (was ~106-112) — DELETED

Numbers/values no longer claimed: `Acid roast + SX $1,430/ton REO`,
`Caustic crack + SX — Higher`, `Chloride route — Variable`.

Reason beyond "unsourced": **the table misrepresents its own citation.**
`@uysal2022economic` (CrossRef abstract checked) compares *beneficiation*
routes at Mountain Pass — direct leaching, attrition scrubbing + leaching, and
flotation + leaching — not acid roast / caustic crack / chloride separation
routes. The three rows are not in the cited paper. Replaced with a paragraph
that says what the paper actually reports (flotation + leaching is the most
profitable; costs are for REO in concentrate, not separated oxide) and a
sentence making the basis problem explicit, which resolves the 10-50×
inconsistency with the $12/kg and $70/kg figures elsewhere in the section.

Also removed from the adjacent (non-table) sensitivity bullets, as part of the
same rewrite: **"1% increase in recovery → ~2% improvement in NPV"** — unsourced
and not in Uysal. What survives is Uysal's actual finding: recovery has a larger
effect on economics than CAPEX or OPEX. Slightly outside the literal M15 scope;
flagging it here.
